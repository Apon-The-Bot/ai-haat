# Legal compliance research register

Research date: 10 November 2026 (Bangladesh time). This is an implementation register, not legal advice or a certification.

| Source / topic | Official source | Current implementation note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumers' Rights Protection Act, 2009 | [Bangladesh Laws, Act 26 of 2009](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1014.html) | Treat truthful advertising, clear product/service descriptions, complaints and remedies as mandatory risk controls. Exact application to each digital product requires counsel. |
| Copyright Act, 2023 | [Bangladesh Laws, Act 34 of 2023](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1452/act-chapter-print-2424.html) and [section 82 source](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1452/act-chapter-print-2425.html) | Product licensing and resale authorization must be evidenced; a disclaimer cannot cure unauthorized distribution. |
| Digital Business Identity (DBID) | [DBID government portal](https://dbid.gov.bd/) | DBID number/status is intentionally not shown because it was not verified in the repository. Obtain and publish only when actually issued and applicable. |
| VAT / tax invoice controls | [NBR VAT Act material](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/18.pdf) and [NBR VAT Rules](https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/rules/Authentic_English_Text_VAT_Rules_2016.pdf) | Tax registration, VAT rate, invoice fields and threshold treatment require accounting confirmation against the actual business status. |
| Digital commerce guidance | [Government policy material referencing the 2021 Guidelines](https://brcp-1.gov.bd/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/National-Digital-Commerce-Policy-2018_10-03-2022.pdf) | The site must not describe the guideline as a complete substitute for current legal advice. Confirm the authoritative current instrument and operational obligations with counsel/Ministry sources. |

## Important unresolved legal questions

- Legal business name, address, trade licence, DBID, TIN/BIN and responsible compliance contact are not verified in the codebase.
- Provider authorization and lawful resale status are not established for each subscription, account, code, credit or workspace product.
- Actual cookie/analytics vendors, retention periods, cross-border transfers and data processor agreements need an operational inventory.
- Refund, replacement, delivery and subscription timelines in product data need reconciliation with public claims before publication.
- The pasted brief references 2026 Bangladesh privacy/cyber statutes; their titles, commencement and applicability must be confirmed from current gazettes or official sources before relying on them.

## Release classification

The Legal Center and draft policy foundation are implemented but awaiting business facts and qualified legal approval. They must not be treated as a claim of full compliance or published binding terms without review.
