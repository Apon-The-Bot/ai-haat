# AI Haat brand voice guidelines

## Positioning

AI Haat is a practical Bangladeshi digital marketplace for AI tools, software subscriptions, VPNs and other digital products. The voice is confident, useful and human—not loud or vague.

## Audience and language

Primary audiences are students, freelancers, developers, creators, entrepreneurs and digital teams. Use concise English for technical terms and natural Bengali for customer guidance. Customer-facing Bengali uses respectful, conversational language consistently.

## Tone by context

- Storefront: energetic and benefit-led.
- Discovery: concise and specific.
- Cart and payment: calm, clear and unambiguous.
- Dashboard: friendly and helpful.
- Referral and affiliate: motivating but transparent; never promise income.
- Support, security and legal: empathetic, direct and precise.
- Admin: operational and concise.

## Rules

Lead with a concrete customer benefit. Explain limits, eligibility and delivery status accurately. Keep familiar labels such as “Add to cart”, “Pay now” and “Sign in”. Avoid unsupported superlatives, fake scarcity, guaranteed earnings, excessive slang, emoji-heavy copy and generic phrases such as “unlock your potential” or “one-stop solution”.

## Examples

- Prefer “তোমার কাজের ডিজিটাল আপগ্রেড এক জায়গায়” over “Discover endless possibilities”.
- Prefer “Payment successful—অর্ডারটি এখন কনফার্মড” over a vague success message.
- Prefer “এখনও কোনো অর্ডার নেই—তোমার প্রয়োজনের টুল খুঁজে নাও” over “No orders found”.
