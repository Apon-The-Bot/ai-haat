# EPS refund and settlement

Manual EPS refund completion goes through the existing Admin approval workflow and requires a provider confirmation reference. No verified refund API contract is available in the supplied guides. Payment transaction IDs are recorded separately from hosted checkout IDs. Merchant settlement fees, net settlement reports, and refund procedures require verification before release.

The [public EPS refund and settlement policy](https://eps.com.bd/public/return-refund-settlement-policy) describes merchant-agreed API or designated email channels for refund requests. It gives an estimated three to five business days for email refunds subject to fund availability and separately describes a seven-working-day claim settlement maximum. It also describes delivery confirmation, including successful access for digital services, as a settlement condition.

Confirm the channel, timing, and digital delivery evidence under AI Haat's signed merchant terms. Keep the EPS confirmation reference in the manual refund record. An approved local refund request does not establish that EPS has paid the customer, and an authorized customer payment does not establish that EPS has settled funds to the merchant.
